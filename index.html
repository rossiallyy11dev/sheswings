About SHE Swings

Designed for leaders, community advocates, and golf enthusiasts, this all-women's tournament supports youth mentorship through Friends of the Children – Portland.

FAQs

Is there a minimum age requirement?
There is no minimum age requirement to attend, and junior golfers are welcome!
Do I need to bring my own golf clubs?
Yes, participants are required to bring their own set of clubs.
