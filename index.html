About SHE Swings

Every swing on the course is a step toward empowering our community. SHE Swings brings together leaders, changemakers, and golf enthusiasts for an exclusive all-women golf and networking experience at Oswego Lake Country Club—supporting the incredible mission of Friends of the Children – Portland.
