About SHE Swings

Designed for leaders, community advocates, and golf enthusiasts, this vibrant charitable tournament supports youth mentorship through Friends of the Children – Portland. SHE Swings combines competitive play, networking, and high-impact fundraising to serve and empower the next generation.

FAQs

Is there a minimum age requirement to attend? +

SHE Swings is open to participants and guests of all ages. However, players registered for full 18-hole championship play must be 18 years or older unless accompanied by an adult guardian.

Do I need to bring my own golf clubs? +

Bringing your own set of clubs is recommended, but rental sets are available through the Oswego Lake Country Club pro shop. Please let us know in advance if you will need rental clubs so we can reserve a set for you.

What is the dress code for the tournament and reception? +

Standard golf attire (collared shirts, soft-spiked golf shoes, slacks or golf skirts) is required on the course. Smart casual attire is recommended for the welcome reception and awards luncheon.

Can I modify or cancel my registration? +

Yes, registration modifications or cancellations can be processed up to 14 days prior to the event. Player substitutions within a registered team are welcome up until the day of the event.

I have additional questions that weren't covered here. Who can I contact? +

For any further questions regarding registration, sponsorships, or general event details, please reach out directly to Mike Lee at mikelee23@mac.com.
