About SHE Swings

Designed for leaders, community advocates, and golf enthusiasts, this vibrant charitable tournament supports youth mentorship through Friends of the Children – Portland. SHE Swings combines competitive play, networking, and high-impact fundraising to serve and empower the next generation.

FAQs

Is there a minimum age requirement to attend? +

There is no minimum age requirement to attend, and junior golfers are welcome to participate!

Do I need to bring my own golf clubs? +

Yes, participants are required to bring their own set of golf clubs.

What is the dress code for the tournament and reception? +

Please wear standard golf apparel appropriate for the course and reception. Denim is not permitted.

Can I modify or cancel my registration? +

Yes, registration details can be modified or canceled. Please email Mike Lee at mikelee23@mac.com to do so.

I have additional questions that weren't covered here. Who can I contact? +

For any further questions regarding registration, sponsorships, or general event details, please reach out directly to Mike Lee at mikelee23@mac.com.
